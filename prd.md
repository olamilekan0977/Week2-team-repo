# CAMPUS LOST AND FOUND MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## OVERVIEW:

The Campus Lost and Found system is a digital platform designed to improve how lost and recovered personal belongings are reported, tracked and returned within a university campus.
Students and university staffs may loose personal belongings in different campus locations , such as lecture halls, libraries, hostels as well as public areas...Therefore finding these belongings can be difficult when the information about the missing and recovered items are scattered across different communication channels or handled through separate offices.

It would have a found category where a person can post a picture and a bit of description of the item and where and when the item was found, It would have a lost category which will help students or university staffs to classify the belongings they have lost or found so that the system can organize reports and make searching easier. Filter tools is also inclusive in the system, it happens whereby the person who lost an item can easily search it based on the categories probably like a wallet, id cards ... then the person who lost the item can easily contact the person who posted the item, set time and location to get it back then the item can be marked as RETURNED.

## PEOPLE POTENTIALLY AFFECTED:

*Students who lose personal belongings
*Students who find belongings
*Security personnel who receive recover items
*University staffs responsible for managing lost properties

## HOW STUDENTS COPE TODAY:

Currently, when students lose personal belongings on campus, they may rely on informal methods to locate and recover them. These methods include:

1. WhatsApp Groups: Students share information about missing or found belongings in class, departmental and other student groups, hoping someone has information about the item.
2. Asking Friends and Classmates: Students ask their friends, roommates and classmates whether they have seen the missing item or know someone who found it.
3. Contacting Class Representatives: Some students may report missing belongings to their class representatives, who can help spread the information to other students.
4. Visiting the Security Office: Students may visit the campus security office to ask whether a missing item has been found or handed over.
5. Making Physical Enquiries: Students sometimes return to places they previously visited, such as lecture halls, libraries, cafeterias and other campus locations, to search for their belongings.

## WHY EXISTING SOLUTION FALL SHORT:

Although these methods can sometimes help students recover their belongings, but they have limitations:

1. Scattered Information: Information about lost and found items is spread across different WhatsApp groups, individuals and offices, making it difficult to locate relevant reports.
2. Difficulty Finding Old Messages: In WhatsApp groups, messages about lost belongings can quickly become buried under newer conversations, making older reports difficult to retrieve.
3. Limited Reach: A student may share information in one group without reaching other students who might have found the missing item.
4. Time-Consuming Searches: Students may spend considerable time visiting different locations or asking several people before finding useful information.
5. Lack of Proper Records: Informal reporting methods may not provide a central record showing which items have been reported, claimed or returned.
6. Difficulty Verifying Ownership: When someone claims a found item, there may not be a consistent process for confirming that the item belongs to that person.

## PROPOSED SOLUTION

The proposed solution is to develop a web-based Campus Lost and Found Management System that provides a centralised platform for students to report, search for and recover lost belongings within the university environment.
The system will allow students to create accounts and submit reports whenever they lose or find an item on campus. Each report will contain important information such as the item name, description, category, location where it was lost or found, date and an optional photograph.
Students will be able to view available reports and use the search and filter features to locate items that match their missing belongings. Filters such as category, location, date and item status will make it easier to narrow down search results.
When a student identifies an item they believe belongs to them, they can submit an ownership claim by providing relevant information about the item. An administrator will review the claim and approve or reject it based on the information provided.
Once ownership has been verified and the item has physically been handed over to its rightful owner, the administrator will update its status to Returned.
The system will also maintain records of reported items, submitted claims and status changes, allowing administrators to manage the recovery process more effectively.

## HOW THE PROPOSED SOLUTION ADDRESSES THE PROBLEM

1. Centralised Reporting: Provides one platform where students can report lost and found belongings instead of depending entirely on scattered communication channels.
2. Easy Searching: Allows students to search for missing belongings without having to ask several people individually.
3. Item Categorisation: Organises reports into categories such as electronics, books, bags, clothing and personal belongings.
4. Filtering: Helps students narrow down search results using location, category, date and status.
5. Ownership Verification: Introduces an administrative process for reviewing claims before belongings are released.
6. Status Tracking: Allows users and administrators to monitor whether an item is lost, found, awaiting verification, claimed or returned.
7. Record Management: Maintains organised records of lost items, found items and ownership claims.

## MINIMUM VIABLE PRODUCT (MVP)

The first version of the Campus Lost and Found Management System will focus on providing the essential functionalities needed to report, locate and recover lost belongings within the university environment.

The MVP will include the following features:

1. User Registration and Login:
   Students will be able to create accounts and log in to the platform. This will help identify users who submit reports and ownership claims.
2. Report Lost Items:
   Students will be able to submit reports about belongings they have lost. Each report will include the item name, description, category, location where it was lost, date and an optional image.
3. Report Found Items:
   Students will be able to report belongings they have found on campus by providing the item’s description, category, location, date and an optional image.
4. Item Categories:
   The system will organise reported belongings into categories such as electronics, books and stationery, bags, clothing and personal belongings.
5. Search and Filter:
   Students will be able to search for reported items and narrow down results using categories, locations, dates and item statuses.
6. Ownership Claim Submission:
   Students who identify an item as theirs will be able to submit a claim and provide information to support their ownership.
7. Administrator Claim Verification:
   An administrator will be able to review ownership claims and approve or reject them based on the information provided.
8. Item Status Management:
   The system will maintain item statuses to show their progress, such as Lost, Found, Pending Verification, Claimed and Returned.
9. Report Management:
   Administrators will be able to view and manage submitted lost-item reports, found-item reports and ownership claims.
   Features Excluded from the First Version:
   The following features will not be included in the initial release but may be considered for future development:

- Live chat between students.
- Mobile application.
- Automated email and SMS notifications.
- Artificial intelligence-based image matching.
- Advanced analytics and reporting.
