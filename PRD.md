                                 CAMPUS LOST AND FOUND MANAGEMENT SYSTEM


DESCRIPTION: The Campus Lost and Found system is a digital platform designed to improve how lost and recovered personal belongings are reported, tracked and returned within a university campus.


Students and university staffs may loose personal belongings in different campus locations , such as lecture halls, libraries, hostels as well as public areas...Therefore finding these belongings can be difficult when the information about the missing and recovered items are scattered across different communication channels or handled through separate offices.


So therefore the proposed system will be like jiji app where students log in their credentials which in this case will be their names and matric number or Using your Babcock email. It would have a found category where a person can post a picture and a bit of description of the item and where and when the item was found, It would have a lost category which will help students or university staffs to classify the belongings they have lost or found so that the system can organize reports and make searching easier. Filter tools is also inclusive in the system, it happens whereby the person who lost an item can easily search it based on the categories probably like a wallet, id cards ... then the person who lost the item can easily contact the person who posted the item, set time and location to get it back then the item can be marked as RETURNED.
